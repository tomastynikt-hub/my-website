JAWA 50 · TYP 23 · 1968–1982

Mustang 50

The little red horse that taught Czechoslovakia to ride.

The legend

Born in Považská Bystrica as the JAWA 50 typ 23, the “Mustang” put a generation on two wheels: a 50 cc two-stroke, three gears shifted by hand, pedals for backup, and that unmistakable long red tank. No license drama, sips fuel, survives everything — the true people's moped.

Specs

Engine49.9 cc two-stroke, air-cooled
Power≈ 3.5 hp (2.6 kW)
Gearbox3-speed, hand shift
Top speed≈ 60–65 km/h
Consumption≈ 2.5 l / 100 km
Weight≈ 68 kg dry

Why riders love it

  • Runs on almost anything and fixes with a screwdriver
  • That sound — a ringing two-stroke echo of summer holidays
  • Values climbing: clean pieces are true collectibles today
